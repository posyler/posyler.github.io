(https://www.pikminwiki.com/Pikipedia:Japanese), lit.: "Courtyard of Sunlight") is the first main area (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Area) in Pikmin 4 (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Pikmin_4), unlocked after completing the tutorial in the Rescue Command Post (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Rescue_Command_Post). It is set in a garden with many pots and bricks. Ice Pikmin (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Ice_Pikmin), Yellow Pikmin (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Yellow_Pikmin) and Blue Pikmin (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Blue_Pikmin) can be discovered in this area. There are 7 rescues (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Castaway) (5 regular castaways and 2 leaflings (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Leafling)) to be made, 34 treasures (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Treasures) (14 above ground and 20 underground), and 2 Onions (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Onion) (a yellow and blue Onion respectively), as well as 2 Flarlic (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Flarlic) bulbs. This area has (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Sparklium) × 2,920 worth of treasure, of which (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Sparklium) × 1,140 is above ground. The next day, the option to travel here in search of Captain Olimar (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Captain_Olimar) and additional Rescue Corps (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Rescue_Corps) members becomes available. As the rookie Rescue Officer (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Rookie_Rescue_Officer) and Oatchi (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Oatchi) explore, they find more treasures (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Treasure) and discover raw material (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Raw_material). They eventually find Russ (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Russ) as the first castaway (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Castaway) rescued. As they keep exploring, they eventually find Schnauz (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Schnauz), a civilian castaway. This brings Shepherd (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Shepherd) to the conclusion that there must be other civilians on the planet (https://www.pikminwiki.com/PNF-404), unlocking a new mission (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Mission_(Pikmin_4)). The duo discovers additional Pikmin types (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Pikmin_family) on their journey, as well as some Onions (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Onion) and Flarlics (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Flarlic). They find a red leafy character (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Leafling) with a big nose, alongside a green Space Dog (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Moss), who are keeping a castaway captive. The following notable features can be found in this area. • As part of the playable terrain: • Various grassy and dirt terrain. • A wooden park bench with metal legs and armrests, though the top of the armrests have a wood slab attached. • A gray stepladder with a beige toolbox on top. • A slab of wood with a water tap coming out of it. • Various walls made of piled-up bricks. • Various colored planks of wood poking up from the ground, arranged together to form walls. • Pool tiles, alternating in a checkerboard pattern between white and blue. • A landing spot identified as "Rugged Scaffold". • A landing spot identified as "Ancient Arches". • As part of the background: • A large iron gate. • Surrounding the large gate are decorated stone walls with brick tops. • Stairs leading from the gate to the playable area. • Shelves with vases and potted plants on them. • A wheelbarrow wheel. • A tall house in the distance with a grey roof. (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Sun-Speckled_Terrace?action=edit§ion=14) The following article or section contains guides (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Pikipedia:Guides) . The strategies shown are just suggestions. This is one of only 2 areas in Pikmin 4 that cannot be 100% completed in 1 day, due to the fact that parts of this area requires Oatchi (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Oatchi) to jump, which can only be unlocked the day after a castaway (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Castaway) has been rescued. On the first day, there are only 2 caves that can be completed: the Last-Frost Cavern (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Last-Frost_Cavern) and Crackling Cauldron (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Crackling_Cauldron) . When you land, you can gather the nearby Pellet Posies (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Pellet_Posy) for some additional Red Pikmin (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Red_Pikmin) , as well as the Dwarf Bulborb (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Dwarf_Bulborb) , and you can find your first instance of raw material (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Raw_material) – important collectibles used to craft gear (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Gear) and build clay (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Clay) structures. Follow the path round until you reach a dirt wall (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Dirt_wall) ; destroy it and turn around until you reach the hectic hollows. Complete the cave and turn left until you reach the next base: the Rugged Scaffold. Deal with the Fiery Blowhog (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Fiery_Blowhog) and move the S.S. Beagle (https://www.pikminwiki.com/S.S._Beagle) over. If you follow the path round, you'll pass some more Dwarf Bulborbs and Bulborbs (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Bulborb) before coming across another dirt wall. Destroy it and enter the cave past it: the Crackling Cauldron, with the Double Dragon-Eyed Scope (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Double_Dragon-Eyed_Scope) sitting on a nearby pot. You could either end the day upon its completion or keep going if you have any more time left. You would've passed the Temporal Mechanism (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Temporal_Mechanism) attached to the fence nearby; it can only be knocked off with a Rush (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Rush) from Oatchi. A bit further past you can see a flarlic (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Flarlic) half-buried on a high-ledge pot. Use your Yellow Pikmin (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Yellow_Pikmin) to dig it up. Two additional treasures can be found in the opposite direction of the Last-Frost Cavern, the Aspiration-Ritual Ball (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Aspiration-Ritual_Ball) on a high ledge and a Lesser Mock Bottom (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Lesser_Mock_Bottom) in some breakable pots (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Breakable_pot) . On the second day, head back to where you found the Temporal Mechanism and climb the nearby slope. Some Male Sheargrubs (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Male_Sheargrub) are nearby, guarding a clay bridge (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Clay_bridge) . Have some Pikmin build it and make your way back to the nearby electric gate (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Electric_gate) , close to the Crackling Cauldron. Destroy it with your Yellow Pikmin and enter the next section of this area, housing the Meandering Slope base. As you make your way to the base, an Iridescent Flint Beetle (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Iridescent_Flint_Beetle) will pop up. A harmless enemy containing a few pellets (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Pellet) . Ideally, you should move your base over here once the bridge you've started construction is complete. You can also find the Satellite Shield (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Satellite_Shield) half-buried on a can nearby, making Yellow Pikmin useful in its collection. Near the moat towards the north, you can find some breakable pots containing a pair of Perforated Rafts (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Perforated_Raft) and a bag (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Bag) blocking access to a Yellow Onion (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Yellow_Onion) . Freeze the moat and you can reach a Dandori Challenge (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Dandori_Challenge) . If you instead climb up the nearby slope from the base, passing some Sheargrubs (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Sheargrub) and a Bulborb, you can find another bridge. Across this bridge is the Dandori Battle (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Dandori_Battle) and a blue- clipboard (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Clipboard) letting you reach the Mysterious Carriage (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Mysterious_Carriage) . Back at the bridge you were originally building, cross it and you can reach the Aquiferous Summit (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Aquiferous_Summit) ; make use of Oatchi's Pick up a Scent command (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Command) to find a nearby buried treasure; the Child of the Earth (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Child_of_the_Earth) . This cave needs Oatchi, as there are small platforms in the way that need him to jump up to it. Beat the cave and exit using the fan on sublevel 2, it'll take you out another exit nearby, at the Distant Summit base. This small section has a Porquillion (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Porquillion) with the Talisman of Life (Moon) (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Talisman_of_Life_(Moon)) inside, and you can also reach the Snack Bean (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Snack_Bean) and Fire-Breathing Feast (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Fire-Breathing_Feast) . The final two caves in this area require Oatchi's jumping ability. One is near the default base Ancient Arches, with the Orb of Destruction (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Orb_of_Destruction) nearby, and the other is near the Rugged Scaffold base, with the Sunseed Berry (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Sunseed_Berry) nearby. The only important collectible left is the Blue Onion (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Blue_Onion) , directly south of the Rugged Scaffold base. Freeze the lake to have some Pikmin build the nearby climbing wall (https://www.pikminwiki.com/Climbing_wall) if you lack Blue Pikmin. Then climb it and transport the Blue Onion across.
