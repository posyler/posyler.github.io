The Blossoming Arcadia (花ふぶきの楽園?, lit.: "Paradise of Falling Flower Petals") is the second main area in Pikmin 4. It is set in a kindergarten playground, with a slide visible in the background. This area has

￼ × 4,878 worth of treasure, of which ￼ × 1,580 is above ground. This area has 60 treasures, there are 20 surface treasures and 40 cave treasures, there are 5 regular castaways and 4 leafling castaways, 2 Onions: a blue Onion and a Flarlic, and there are 7 caves (4 regular, 2 Dandori Challenges and 1 Dandori Battle). After collecting ￼ × 1,500, the S.S. Shepherd is repaired slightly and its radar's range is increased. The radar now picks up SOS signals in this new area. The rookie Rescue Officer and Oatchi set off to this new area in search of Captain Olimar, the remaining Rescue Corps members, other civilian castaways and treasure. They may also be in search of the red leafling and other leaflings if encountered in the Sun-Speckled Terrace. The duo encounter Rock Pikmin for the first time (in the Kingdom of Beasts), and possibly Blue Pikmin if their Onion has been retrieved in the Sun-Speckled Terrace. They manage to collect a couple of Flarlic and a blue Onion, granting them additional blue Pikmin. They also manage to rescue a few castaways and leaflings, and enough treasures to upgrade the S.S. Shepherd once more, unlocking the next area — the Serene Shores. The Blossoming Arcadia is an open, spring-themed playground. There are multiple toy-like structures in this area, such as wooden blocks, upturned toys and toy treasures, such as the Shake-a-Smile and the inflatable yo-yo. Multiple cherry blossom trees can be found around in this area, with a large one located toward the south; cherry petals can be found on the ground near these trees. A shallow body of water is located in the center of the area, with multiple water-related enemies found nearby, such as the Wollyhop, Leaves and Creeping Chrysanthemums. This area is also full of multiple different types of flowers, along with different types of Spectralids and a Mamuta. The following notable features can be found in this area.

  • Some blocks along the floor.
  • An upturned yellow toy.
  • A small wooden model bucket.
